Cookie Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit them. Cookies allow websites to remember your actions and preferences, such as your login status, language selection, font size, and other display preferences, so you don't need to re-enter this information every time you return to the website or browse different pages.

2. How We Use Cookies

LIEN Photography uses Cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Ensure the website functions properly
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Improve user experience
  • Analyze website traffic and usage
  • Provide personalized content
  • Ensure website security

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Essential Cookies

These Cookies are essential for the basic functionality of the website and cannot be turned off. They are usually only set in response to actions you take, such as setting privacy preferences, logging in, or filling out forms.

Examples: Firebase authentication Cookies, session management Cookies

3.2 Functional Cookies

These Cookies allow the website to remember choices you make (such as your username, language, or region) and provide enhanced and personalized features.

Examples: Language preferences, display settings, theme selection

3.3 Analytics Cookies

These Cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the website, such as which pages are most popular, how long visitors stay on the website, etc. This information helps us improve the website.

Examples: Firebase Analytics Cookies, Google Analytics Cookies

6. How to Manage Cookies

You can manage or delete Cookies in the following ways:

6.1 Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View Cookies you have and delete them
  • Block third-party Cookies
  • Block Cookies from specific websites
  • Delete all Cookies when closing the browser
  • Block all Cookies

Please note that blocking certain Cookies may affect website functionality.

6.2 Browser Instructions

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and Website Data
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions > Cookies and Site Data

6.3 Google Analytics Opt-out

If you want to opt out of Google Analytics tracking, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

7. Cookies on Mobile Devices
