Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to LIEN Photography (hereinafter referred to as "this website"). We highly value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

2. Data Collection

2.1 Types of Data We Collect

When you use this website, we may collect the following types of data:

  • Contact Information: When you contact us through the contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and other information.
  • Usage Data: We collect how you use this website, including pages visited, time spent, click behavior, etc.
  • Technical Data: Including IP address, browser type, operating system, device information, etc.
  • Photo Data: If you upload photos, we store photo files and related information (such as title, description, shooting date, etc.).
  • Authentication Data: We use Firebase anonymous authentication to identify users but do not collect your personal identification information.

2.2 Methods of Data Collection

  • Data you actively provide (such as contact forms, photo uploads)
  • Automatically collected technical data (such as Cookies, browser information)
  • Data provided by third-party services (such as Firebase Analytics)

3. Purpose of Data Use

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • Providing and improving this website's services
  • Responding to your inquiries and requests
  • Processing photo uploads, classification, and management
  • Using AI technology for photo classification (using Google Gemini API)
  • Analyzing website usage to improve user experience
  • Ensuring website security and preventing fraudulent behavior
  • Complying with legal obligations

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Data Storage

Your data is stored in the following services:

  • Firebase Storage: For storing uploaded photo files
  • Firestore: For storing photo metadata and related information
  • EmailJS: For processing contact form email sending
  • Google Gemini API: For AI photo classification services

4.2 Data Security Measures

We take the following security measures to protect your data:

  • Using encryption technology to protect data transmission
  • Implementing access control and authentication mechanisms
  • Regular data backups
  • Using reputable third-party service providers (Google Firebase)
  • Restricting data access to authorized personnel only

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. We only share your data in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Sharing necessary data with third-party service providers (such as Firebase, EmailJS, Google) that help us operate the website
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Consent: With your explicit consent

6. Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services, which may have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: For data storage, authentication, and analytics services
  • Google Gemini API: For AI photo classification
  • EmailJS: For email sending services

We recommend that you review the privacy policies of these services to understand how they handle your data.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This website uses Cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience, analyze website traffic, and provide personalized content. For detailed information, please refer to our Cookie Policy.

8. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection regulations, you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request to view your personal data held by us
  • Right of Rectification: Request correction of incorrect or incomplete data
  • Right of Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request restriction of our processing of your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Request your data in a structured format
  • Right to Object: Object to our processing of your data

To exercise these rights, please contact us through the contact form or email.

9. Data Retention

We will retain your data until:

  • You request deletion of data
  • Data is no longer needed to provide services
  • The legal retention period expires

10. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we discover that we have collected data from children, we will delete it immediately.

11. International Data Transfer

Your data may be transferred to and stored on servers outside your country/region. We will ensure appropriate security measures are taken to protect your data.

12. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We recommend that you review this policy regularly to stay informed.
